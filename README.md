🏠 Khatiyan is a modular property and tenancy management platform built for PGs, hostels, and rental operators who want to move beyond spreadsheets, WhatsApp chats, and manual rent tracking.

It brings the most important rental workflows into one backend system:

🔐 Auth & onboarding for owners, tenants, and property managers
🏢 Property and room management with occupancy tracking
🤝 Tenancy lifecycle management from check-in to exit
💸 Billing cycles, deposits, late fees, discounts, and adjustments
⚡ Razorpay payment integration with idempotency and webhook handling
📢 Notices and recurring announcements for property-level communication
🛠️ Concerns/ticket management for tenant issues and resolution tracking
🔔 In-app notification pipeline ready for push-provider integration
⏱️ Schedulers and background jobs for recurring notices, billing, late fees, and future workflows
🧱 Modular monolith architecture using Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, Flyway, Valkey, and clean module boundaries
The goal of Khatiyan is to provide a reliable backend foundation for digitizing daily PG/rental operations: managing rooms, tenants, rent, deposits, exits, notices, complaints, and payments in one structured system.
